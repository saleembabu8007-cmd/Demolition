# Azi Engineering Works — Vector Logo Kit

This kit rebuilds the user-supplied raster artwork as clean, **native SVG vectors**. No raster/embedded screenshots, no external linked images, and no font files.

## Official colors (matched/cleaned from the reference)
- Deep navy: **#112A3E**
- Warm gold: **#FFB800**
- Reversed logo: **#FFFFFF** + **#FFB800** on a dark background

## Assets
1. Primary stacked in brand colors — websites, letterheads, presentations
2. Horizontal lockup in brand colors — website headers, signage
3. Compact mark + name — small signage, banners
4. Symbol only — watermark, stamps, small-brand applications
5. Reversed stacked — dark backgrounds
6. Reversed horizontal — dark backgrounds
7. One-color navy — one-ink stationery
8. One-color black — monochrome printing
9. One-color white — single-color reversed use
10. Social badge on navy — profile and app/social icon
11. Social badge on white — profile and app/social icon
12. Wordmark only — secondary lettering application
13. Editable-text master — source with editable text (uses Inter Display font, with fallback)

## How to edit
- SVG versions 01–12 have outlined vector lettering: they will look consistent even without any installed font. Outlined text must be edited as paths; these are the safest production masters.
- SVG 13 has editable, live text and path-based symbol geometry. To preserve the intended typography, use **Inter Display ExtraBold** for Azi and **Inter Display Medium** for Engineering Works. For final production, convert text to outlines.
- Reversed files (05, 06, 09) have **transparent backgrounds** and contain white shapes; place them on dark surfaces to view them properly.
- Keep the logo proportions unchanged and maintain generous clear space around the emblem and lettering. Suggested minimum clear space is at least half the width of the vertical navy leg of the icon.

## Notes
The logo is a faithful visual cleanup and recreation, not a trace of an original vector or an assertion of trademark clearance. Proportions, geometry, crispness, spacing, and type consistency were refined. The small raster source doesn't permit an exact recovery of the original font.
